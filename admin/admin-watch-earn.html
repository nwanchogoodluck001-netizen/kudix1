Reward Security

Important production requirements

Server-side watch validation
Verify viewing sessions on the server. Do not rely on client-side timers or browser events to approve payments.
Duplicate claim protection
Enforce one eligible claim per user per video, or the explicit claim limit configured by your business rules.
Wallet ledger
Credit the user's wallet only after the backend validates eligibility. Record a unique transaction reference for every reward.
Administrative permissions
Protect uploads, reward changes, publication, deletion, and reporting behind authenticated admin permissions.
