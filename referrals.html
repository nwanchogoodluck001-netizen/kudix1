Referral rules & transparency

Please understand the requirements before sharing.

  • Only referrals that meet the official eligibility criteria can qualify for rewards.
  • Duplicate accounts, fraudulent activity, or invalid referrals may be rejected in line with platform rules.
  • Some referrals may remain pending while eligibility and account activity are reviewed.
  • The ₦1,000 reward is conditional, not guaranteed income. Actual rewards depend on the official program terms and verification.
  • Never share passwords, verification codes, or sensitive account information with anyone.
