Questions?
We've Got Answers.

Find helpful information about getting started, game access, promotional credit, and referrals.

Still need help?

Open the KUDIX support panel for available support options.

What is KUDIX?

KUDIX is a gaming and rewards platform featuring prediction games, referral activities, and eligible social tasks.

How do I create an account?

Select Get Started to open the registration page. Complete the registration process when the account system is available.

What is the ₦20,000 welcome bonus?

It is promotional gaming credit for eligible gameplay, subject to the platform's applicable terms. It is not withdrawable cash.

Can I withdraw my welcome gaming credit?

No. The ₦20,000 welcome credit is promotional gaming credit and must be kept separate from withdrawable funds.

How do I unlock access to games?

You must meet the requirement of three eligible referrals before accessing restricted games. Check the applicable game rules for details.

How does the referral programme work?

Use your unique referral link to invite people. A referral must satisfy the platform's eligibility rules before it qualifies.

When is a referral considered successful?

A referral qualifies when the required registration and eligibility checks have been completed under the platform's referral rules.

How do social tasks work?

Complete an available task and submit any requested proof. Rewards depend on task requirements and approval.

Where can I find official KUDIX announcements?

Use the official Telegram or WhatsApp buttons when the platform's public channel links have been configured.

How can I contact customer support?

Open the floating support button to view the available support topics. Direct agent assistance requires a configured support service.
