Quick guide

How to play Higher or Lower

Four simple steps to understand the number challenge.

01

Observe the number

Check the current number displayed in the game area.

02

Make your choice

Choose Higher if you predict a greater number, or Lower if you predict a smaller number.

03

Reveal the result

Wait for the new number to be generated and revealed.

04

Check your result

See whether your prediction matched the direction of the number change.

Remember: Equal numbers are possible. If both numbers are the same, the result is recorded as “Same Number,” not as a correct or incorrect prediction. Numbers are generated independently for each demo draw, so previous results do not predict the next one.
